      Collab guide

      Run your raffle.

      1. Apply with your community. We review every application by hand and DM you on X.
      2. Get your spots. Once approved, this page shows how many FheCat allowlist (WL) spots you have and a timer to hand them out.
      3. Run a raffle or a giveaway in your community. Use the post below or your own.
      4. Add the winners here: a name (Discord or X) and an EVM wallet (0x…) per person. Save as you go.
      5. Submit before the timer ends. Submitted wallets go on the FheCat allowlist for the mainnet mint.

      Rules

      • One person per spot, one wallet per person.
      • EVM wallets only (they start with 0x). No exchange deposit addresses.
      • No bots, no farms. We may remove wallets that look like either.
      • After you submit, the list is locked. DM @FheCat if you need a change.
