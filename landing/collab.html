      Collab guide

      Run your raffle.

      1. Apply with your community and a post about FheCat on its X. We review every application by hand and DM you on X.
      2. Get your spots. Once approved, this page shows how many FheCat allowlist (WL) spots you have and a 48-hour timer, which starts at approval, to submit your winners.
      3. Post your raffle on your community's X. Use the post below or your own, with @FheCat in it.
      4. Add the winners here: a name (Discord or X) and an EVM wallet (0x…) per person. Save as you go.
      5. Submit with the link to your raffle post (and your AlphaBot link, if you ran it there), before the timer ends. Submitted wallets go on the FheCat allowlist for the mainnet mint.

      Rules

      • One X account can send up to 2 applications.
      • The raffle runs in public, in a post on X. We need its link to take your list.
      • One person per spot, one wallet per person.
      • EVM wallets only (they start with 0x). No exchange deposit addresses.
      • No bots, no farms. We may remove wallets that look like either.
      • After you submit, the list is locked. DM @FheCat if you need a change.
