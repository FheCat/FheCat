01 · Born encrypted

Every cat is a secret.

FheCat is a collection of pixel cats with a twist: every cat's traits are encrypted the moment it's minted. The contract only ever holds them in encrypted form.

So there's nothing to snipe and nothing to leak. The whole thing is designed so nobody can peek: not bots, not snipers, not even us. Only the owner can decrypt their own cat. Everyone else meets it at the reveal.

02 · How FHE works

Math on locked boxes.

Fully Homomorphic Encryption (FHE) lets a computer work on data without ever decrypting it. Fhenix brings FHE to Ethereum: with its CoFHE coprocessor, smart contracts on EVM chains can add, compare and choose between encrypted numbers.

  1. 1 Encrypted in3 and 4 are encrypted on your device before they ever reach the chain.
  2. 2 Computed blindCoFHE runs FHE.add on the ciphertexts. It never sees a 3, a 4 or a 7.
  3. 3 Opened by youThe result stays encrypted. Only an address it's been shared with (FHE.allow) can decrypt it.

FheCat is built on the same idea: traits will be rolled from encrypted random numbers (FHE.randomEuint8 and friends) and stay sealed until the reveal.
